# Sidequest

A conference copilot for brains that cannot hold the whole schedule in their head.

Built solo at the Cloud Native Hackathon, KubeCon + CloudNativeCon North America 2026, Salt Lake City, Monday 9 November 2026. Challenge: make conferences easier.

You give Sidequest your interests and your limits (for example "LLM inference, edge AI, people to meet; I need breaks"). It reads the official KubeCon schedule and every Luma side event, finds your talks with Elasticsearch hybrid search, and returns one low-overwhelm plan for the day: buffers between sessions, one quiet break per half day, clashes flagged with the alternative, and a "too much, simplify" button that drops the day to three items. For side events it can hand the registration to an agent on your own phone, which fills the form and stops with the button on screen for you to press.

The planner is deliberately deterministic. A plan that changes every time you look at it is itself a cognitive load.
